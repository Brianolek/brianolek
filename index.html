About

I paint American life as it is.

Mostly people at work: men in suits, the office, the commute, the middle of the day. The cars are the same subject at a different speed.

The paintings are oil on canvas, made in New York. The figures come out of black because that is how they arrive: a face, a collar, a tie, and the rest still forming.

Brian Olek lives and works in New York.
